How the engine decides

  • Resolution override — a populated Resol field (incl. 0) forces that exact value over everything (Q2, Q3).
  • Otherwise charge max(frontage, building, consumption) — the largest driver wins.
  • Frontage → units: ≤500 ft → ft÷100; >500 ft → 5 + (ft−500)÷200 (Q1).
  • Consumption: Σ(last 4 reads) ÷ 75,000 (Q4) — here read from the precomputed value.
  • Blank district → not charged for that tax (shows n/a).
