    Why does a payment take time to display? (plain-English)

    When a customer pays online, their bank approves the charge right away and the money is captured. Almost always, that approval flows straight back into our system and shows on the account within seconds.

    Once in a while, that final hand-off hiccups: the bank already has the money, but our screen didn't get the confirmation, so the payment hasn't finished showing on the customer's account yet. Nothing is lost — the funds are captured and safe.

    We catch these and post them, and we're working with our payment provider to stop the hiccup from happening. That's what "catching up on display" means on this page.
