# About

I'm Nathaniel Genwright — a Product Owner / technical business analyst with ~20 years across government software, payments, and software QA. Day to day, I'm the subject-matter expert that developers and clients route their hardest problems through.

What I've come to realize is that repetitive manual work is mostly un-codified process — and codifying it into a tool improves my throughput, accuracy, and consistency at the same time, while freeing my attention for the judgment calls that actually need a human. I build these tools with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) and the Model Context Protocol; this repo collects a couple of them as sanitized case studies.

## A few principles

- **Process as product.** A painful recurring process deserves the same design attention as a feature. Treat it like one.
- **Automate the assembly, not the judgment.** The most valuable automations leave a human in control of the decision and take the busywork off their plate.
- **Verify, don't just collect.** Whether it's a release note or a UI test, the value is in checking that something is actually true — not just gathering it.
- **Legible by default.** Plain-English tests, controlled vocabularies, human-readable outputs. If only the author can read it, it isn't done.

## Sanitization

Public, third-party tools are named (JIRA, Microsoft Teams, Playwright, Claude Code). What's withheld is **employer-specific data**: the company and customer names, real ticket contents, and the internal systems under test. The approaches and integrations are real; the proprietary data is not.
